# Support

Start with the [complete animation workflow](docs/animation-workflow.md),
especially its input-contract and troubleshooting sections.

For reproducible bugs, open a
[bug report](https://github.com/ReidSurmeier/plotter-line-drawing-svg/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
For workflow questions or focused improvements, open a regular issue and include
the command, environment, alpha-stack shape, ink count, and the relevant
metadata or animation manifest.

This is a small maintainer-led project, so response times are not guaranteed.
Never put secrets, private Tailscale URLs, confidential images, or unlicensed
media in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.
